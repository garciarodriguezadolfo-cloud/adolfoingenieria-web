ENGINEERING · TECHNICAL ANALYSIS

Second occupancy certificates.

Technical review and documentation for the second occupancy procedure for homes in Alicante province, according to the property and municipal requirements.

Request a quotation

01

Reviewing the property's circumstances

Before preparing a second occupancy certificate, available records, residential use and the council's requirements are reviewed. A visit enables the relevant technical checks; any issues identified are explained so that the next steps can be assessed.

02

Requirements vary between municipalities

The procedure name, required documents and submission process may differ. An occupancy responsible declaration or other technical documentation may be required. The service does not guarantee approval or by itself regularise non-compliant works or uses.

03

Documents to prepare

Provide the address, cadastral reference if available, plans and previous occupancy documents, together with details of alterations. The quotation defines the review, visit and technical documentation; submission, fees and any additional work must be expressly agreed.

04

Coverage across Alicante province

Service in Benidorm, Altea, Villajoyosa, Calpe, Dénia, Benissa, Polop, La Nucía, Callosa d’en Sarrià and l’Alfàs del Pi, and other Alicante municipalities. If an energy certificate is also needed, each assignment is assessed with its own scope.
