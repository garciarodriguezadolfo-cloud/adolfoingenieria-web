Second occupancy certificates in Alicante.

Technical assessment and documents for the Spanish segunda ocupación procedure in Alicante province, Marina Baixa and the Costa Blanca. The property and the relevant council’s procedure are reviewed before defining the work.

Request a quotation

01

Reviewing the property's circumstances

Before preparing a second occupancy certificate, available records, residential use and the council's requirements are reviewed. A visit enables the relevant technical checks; any issues identified are explained so that the next steps can be assessed.

02

Habitability documents and municipal procedures

If you are looking for a habitability certificate or occupancy licence, the first step is to identify the documents and procedure required by the local council. Those terms do not make every Spanish segunda ocupación process equivalent. An occupancy declaration and specific technical documents may be required. A technical certificate does not replace any necessary administrative decision or legalise non-compliant works or use.

03

Documents to prepare

Provide the address, cadastral reference if available, plans and previous occupancy documents, together with details of alterations. The quotation defines the review, visit and technical documentation; submission, fees and any additional work must be expressly agreed.

04

Alicante, Benidorm, Altea and Costa Blanca coverage

Second occupancy enquiries are reviewed in Benidorm, Altea, Villajoyosa, Calpe, Dénia, Benissa, La Nucía, Polop, Callosa d’en Sarrià and l’Alfàs del Pi, across Marina Baixa, the Costa Blanca and Alicante province. Council procedures are checked individually rather than assuming identical paperwork. If an energy certificate is also required, each assignment is assessed separately.
