Accident investigation and reconstruction.

Understanding an accident requires evidence and a coherent technical analysis. Available throughout Spain, particularly in the Valencian Community and Alicante.

Request a quotation

01

Experience in investigation and cause analysis

Experience in accident investigation and report writing supports a fact-focused approach. The sequence of events, observed damage and relevant mechanical factors are examined to assess possible causes and their compatibility with the evidence.

02

Reconstruction based on available evidence

Technical reconstruction draws on photographs, plans, measurements, documents and inspections where appropriate. Hypotheses are checked against available data. Missing evidence and uncertainties are made explicit; a particular conclusion cannot be guaranteed.

03

A report with a traceable line of reasoning

The report sets out the purpose, sources, method, analysis and conclusions. It may form part of an expert opinion and include court testimony if agreed within the assignment. Feasibility is reviewed case by case within the scope of mechanical engineering.

04

Preserve information from the outset

Provide the date and location, a chronology, original photographs, existing reports and details of the items involved. Do not take risks to obtain evidence or alter items without documenting their condition. Mention any known legal or claims deadlines.
