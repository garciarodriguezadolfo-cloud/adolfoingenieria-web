Business opening and ownership changes.

Technical support for starting a business activity or reviewing a change of operator in Alicante province. The procedure depends on the activity, premises and municipal requirements.

Request a quotation

01

Before opening or taking over premises

Reviewing administrative records and technical conditions before committing to an investment helps identify potential issues. The proposed use, existing documentation and applicable requirements are examined to establish what needs to be prepared or checked.

02

Opening licences and responsible declarations

Activities do not all follow the same procedure. An opening licence, responsible declaration or another process may apply. Documentation may include technical projects and reports within the applicable professional scope. Feasibility and the extent of the work are confirmed after reviewing the assignment.

03

Changes of activity ownership

A change of operator should not be treated as permission to alter the activity or premises. Existing records and any changes affecting authorised conditions are reviewed. Submission work, fees and documents are defined according to council requirements.

04

Information for a quotation

Provide the municipality, address, intended activity, approximate floor area, condition of the premises and any existing licence or technical documents. Service covers Alicante province; timing depends on technical work and administrative procedures, and approval cannot be guaranteed.
