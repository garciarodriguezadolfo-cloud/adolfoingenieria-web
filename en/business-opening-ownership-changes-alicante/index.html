Business opening and operator changes on the Costa Blanca.

Technical support for opening commercial premises and changing business operators on the Costa Blanca, with particular attention to the municipalities of Marina Baixa.

Requirements and procedures are assessed according to the activity, the characteristics of the premises and the applicable municipal regulations.

Request a quotation

01

Before opening or taking over premises

Reviewing administrative records and technical conditions before committing to an investment helps identify potential issues. The proposed use, existing documentation and applicable requirements are examined to establish what needs to be prepared or checked.

02

Opening licences, activity declarations and technical projects

Engineering support for a Spanish opening licence starts with the activity and premises. The competent authority may require an opening licence, a declaración responsable de actividad or another procedure, with a technical project, report or certificate as applicable. This Spanish declaration is not an automatic equivalent of business permits in other countries. Documents and feasibility are defined within the applicable professional scope; approval is not guaranteed.

03

Changes of activity ownership

A change of operator should not be treated as permission to alter the activity or premises. Existing records and any changes affecting authorised conditions are reviewed. Submission work, fees and documents are defined according to council requirements.

04

Information for a quotation

Provide the municipality, address, intended activity, approximate floor area, condition of the premises and any existing licence or technical documents. Service covers the Costa Blanca, with particular attention to Marina Baixa; timing depends on technical work and administrative procedures, and approval cannot be guaranteed.
