Energy performance certificates on the Costa Blanca.

Preparation and registration of Spanish energy performance certificates (CEE) for homes and other properties on the Costa Blanca, with particular attention to the municipalities of Marina Baixa.

Technical advice on energy certification for properties intended for sale or rental, in accordance with current legislation.

Request a quotation

01

What the energy assessment covers

The work starts with property information and a visit to record relevant characteristics, including the building envelope, openings and systems. The certifying technician assesses energy performance using the applicable procedure and prepares the certificate with its rating and improvement recommendations.

02

Documents and registration

An initial quotation requires the location, use, approximate floor area and available documents, such as plans or previous certificates. The quotation should specify the visit, certificate preparation and any agreed registration work and fees. A rating or deadline cannot be promised without reviewing the property.

03

Energy certificates on the Costa Blanca

I prepare energy performance certificates throughout the Costa Blanca, with particular attention to the municipalities of Marina Baixa.

The service includes the site visit, the energy assessment of the property, preparation of the certificate and its registration with the competent authority.

04

When to enquire

Before a sale or rental, check whether a certificate is required, its validity and any case-specific exemptions. Energy certification does not replace occupancy documentation. The second occupancy service may also be relevant.
