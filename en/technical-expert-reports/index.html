Mechanical assessments and technical reports

02

Expert opinions and court testimony

An expert opinion can support a claim or legal or non-legal proceedings. Where required by the assignment, court testimony and explanation of the findings can be considered. Acceptance depends on the subject, evidence and applicable professional scope; no court appointment is implied.
