01

Preliminary technical assessment: causes and feasibility

A preliminary technical report reviews the facts, indications of mechanical or technical failure and the technical feasibility of the questions raised. It distinguishes supportable findings from uncertainties and identifies further checks that may be needed. It can be the first phase of an assignment that later develops into a full expert report, but that progression is not automatic: it requires a fresh assessment and an expressly agreed scope.

02

Expert witness reports for Spanish courts

Manuel Adolfo García Rodríguez, mechanical engineer, prepares expert witness reports and technical opinions within the accepted professional scope. A report intended for court sets out sources, method, analysis and conclusions, separating established facts from hypotheses. Giving evidence and explaining the report in court can be included where appropriate and agreed. Technical support does not replace legal advice or imply appointment by a Spanish court.

03

Engineering expert witness services throughout Spain

I prepare technical expert reports and expert opinions for court proceedings throughout Spain, with particular attention to the Valencian Community.

Each assignment is assessed individually to determine its scope, the technical work required and the feasibility of the investigation, ensuring an objective, independent and technically substantiated analysis.

04

Documents for an initial scope review

Prepare a chronology, original photographs, contracts, invoices, previous reports and the technical questions to be answered. Mention the stage of any proceedings and known deadlines, without sending sensitive material through the initial form. Inspections, measurements or other checks are assessed before agreeing the assignment. Accident investigation and reconstruction has its own service page.
